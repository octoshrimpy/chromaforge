# DM toolbox

Inspiration the generators don't already cover. Forge runs on improvisation: use any part, or none.

Biomes, tile content, and landmarks live in [world tile](../generators/world_tile.md) and [landmark](../generators/landmark.md). Only leftovers are kept here.

## Leftover biomes

Alternates for the world-tile table: snowy peaks, frozen forest (yellow). Flooded canyon, water caves (blue). Burned wasteland (purple). Lava geyser field (red). Flower fields, orchards (green).

## Landmark twists

- Offensive statue: d6 label: ugliest, dumbest, most indecisive, most incompetent, most ambitious, most clumsy.
- Changeling fountain: once anyone draws fluid out, everyone sees that fluid.

## Puzzles

Describe, don't roll. Use checks only if players attempt risky actions.

**For an item**

- **Greedy pool**: siphon cup, acid (or deep) pool, item at the bottom.
- **Weighted scale**: item on the ceiling. Add weights to raise a party member.
- **Sequenced journal**: a number sequence hidden in the room plus scattered numbered pages. Bind them in order and place on the pedestal.
- **Portal pool cup**: bowls of water act as linked portals. Reach in one, out the other, to grab items or press buttons.

**For an entryway**

- **Fake countdown**: counts down 1d10+10 turns and does nothing. The door opens one turn after it ends.
- **Say the password**: "password is X" is engraved above the door. Get the door to say it.
- **Floor plates**: step on tiles 1–6 in a random order.
- **Turn the statue**: three closed doorways. Attack the statue and it hits back once. Turn it toward the right door (1d4 − 1). Wrong doors open to nothing.

**To develop**

- **Mirror and light**: bounce a beam through 1+ mirrors onto the target.
- **Show me only wealth**: a pedestal face reads "show me only X". Cover "show me only" so it sees only X. Red-herring items in the room.
- Traps: none yet.

## V1 monster pool

Generic fantasy creatures from before the [monster generator](../generators/monster.md) moved to Katharra fauna. Use for non-canon runs or to reskin.

- **Ranged**: bat, hawk, eagle, acolyte, flying sword, guard (calls reinforcements), sentient locust swarm, faerie/baby/adult dragon
- **Arcana**: cultist, hag, vampire (shapeshifter, magic-immune, spawn), elemental, archmage, lich
- **Stealth**: snake, cat, bandit, deep gnome, tiger, assassin, troglodyte, sabertooth, gnoll, sahuagin
- **Strength**: commoner, zombie, thug, knight, bugbear, gladiator, gargoyle, hobgoblin, cyclops
- **Social**: ape, baboon, scarecrow, doppelganger, awakened tree, intellect devourer, sphinx, beholder, tengu
- **Beasts**: badger, banshee, brown/black bear, boar, crab, crocodile, wolf, direwolf, octopus, wasp
- **Mythical**: basilisk, wyrm, giant, griffon, harpy, hellhound, hippogriff, hydra, manticore, kraken, geist, wyvern, chimera, gorgon
- **Magical**: slime, drake, gelatinous cube, reanimated skeleton, crawling claw, drow, ettercap, iron golem, mummy, owlbear, shadow, specter, ghost, wight, mimic, homunculus
- **Gigantism** (huge version of a small thing): slug, slime, snail, spider, beetle, rat, centipede, bat, crab, frog, lizard, octopus, scorpion, wasp, scarab
