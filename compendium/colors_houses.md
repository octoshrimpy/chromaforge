# Colors and the Five Founts

This is the active color and lore reference. Alpha characters have all five Founts as stats; Houses, fixed focus/sense links, and class ownership are V1 mechanics, not Fount identities. Founts are laws of being without agency. Worldbuilding source: [The Five Founts](../../katharra.wiki/magic/the_five_founts.md).

## The five

| Fount     | Domain    | Color        | Felt tone                |
| --------- | --------- | ------------ | ------------------------ |
| **Seron** | Integrity | `YEL` yellow | relief at definition     |
| **Orvik** | Knowledge | `BLU` blue   | curious but humble       |
| **Zhark** | Change    | `RED` red    | thrill and unease        |
| **Drun**  | Fortitude | `GRN` green  | protective ache          |
| **Veyra** | Charm     | `PRP` purple | warm bonds, jealous edge |

Use this stable order when a table needs one: `YEL → BLU → RED → GRN → PRP`.

White and black remain historical labels only. Active yellow means Seron / Integrity; active purple means Veyra / Charm.

## Seron — Integrity · Yellow

_Titles: The Golden Line · The Unbroken Measure · They-Who-Define · The Keeper of Boundaries_

Order, lawful, by the book. **Domain:** sight, judgment, the line between true and false. **Use:** name it and hold it — set borders, make lines clear; to declare a boundary is to make it absolute. **Cost:** thought stiffens if overused; nuance fades until rested.

## Orvik — Knowledge · Blue

_Titles: The Silent Archive · The Ink Beneath the Sea · They-Who-Remember · The Hidden Eye_

Wisdom, intelligence. **Domain:** pattern, memory, hidden structure. **Use:** bind effects with pattern — proofs, memories, maps; a clear theorem can close a door, a gentle paradox can loosen a knot. **Cost:** attention narrows; you set one thing down to hold another. Rest brings breadth back.

## Zhark — Change · Red

_Titles: The Crimson Maw · The Shatter-Song · They-Who-Break · The Red Tempest_

Rupture, mischief, volatility. **Domain:** passion, the twist, the swap, the break. **Use:** bend outcomes, skew odds, open small ruptures that lead to new paths. **Cost:** the self becomes less predictable; habits slip. Slow work and re-centering restore grip.

## Drun — Fortitude · Green

_Titles: The Verdant Bastion · The Rooted Mountain · They-Who-Endure · The Stone Beneath All_

Strength, endurance, the will to persist. **Domain:** growth, cycles, keeping on. **Use:** bind to cycles, turn pain into fuel, keep going and grow back stronger. **Cost:** rigidity of choices; small signals get missed. The price is in perception and flexibility, never the body.

## Veyra — Charm · Purple

_Titles: The Veiled Smile · The Violet Thread · They-Who-Entangle · The Perfumed Mask_

Social, kinship, the pull between beings. **Domain:** desire, bonds, alignment. **Use:** tie and untie bonds, align groups, lean hearts with words and presence. **Cost:** each tie tugs back; autonomy thins if you live only in others. Solitude restores self.

## The Star

Every mortal carries all five Founts as fragments, marked by a **five-pointed star** birthmark near the navel — one point per fragment, its position random at birth, drifting with practice. A star resting dead-center (the solar plexus) is **Centered**: balanced across all five.

## How the Founts interlock

- Seron's absolutes clash with Zhark's ruptures.
- Orvik's patterns often rely on Seron's definitions.
- Drun's endurance resists Veyra's sway.
- Veyra's tethers bend both Seron's laws and Zhark's chaos.
