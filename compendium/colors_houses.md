# Colors and the Five Founts

This is the active color and lore reference. Alpha characters have all five Founts as stats; Houses, fixed focus/sense links, and class ownership are V1 mechanics, not Fount identities. Founts are laws of being without agency. Worldbuilding source: [The Five Founts](../../katharra.wiki/magic/the_five_founts.md).

## The five

| Fount       | Domain    | Color        |
| ----------- | --------- | ------------ |
| **ᛄ Seron** | Integrity | `YEL` yellow |
| **∵ Orvik** | Knowledge | `BLU` blue   |
| **ϟ Zhark** | Spark     | `RED` red    |
| **△ Drun**  | Fortitude | `GRN` green  |
| **⌘ Veyra** | Charm     | `PRP` purple |

Use this stable order when a table needs one: `YEL → BLU → RED → GRN → PRP`.

---

## ᛄ Seron — Integrity · Yellow

_Titles: The Golden Line · The Unbroken Measure · They-Who-Define · The Keeper of Boundaries_

> Order, lawful, by the book. 
**Domain:** sight, judgment, the line between true and false. 
**Use:** name it and hold it — set borders, make lines clear; to declare a boundary is to make it absolute. 

## ∵ Orvik — Knowledge · Blue

_Titles: The Silent Archive · The Ink Beneath the Sea · They-Who-Remember · The Hidden Eye_

> Wisdom, intelligence. 
**Domain:** pattern, memory, hidden structure. 
**Use:** bind effects with pattern — proofs, memories, maps; a clear theorem can close a door, a gentle paradox can loosen a knot. 

## ϟ Zhark — Spark · Red

_Titles: The Crimson Maw · The Shatter-Song · They-Who-Break · The Red Tempest_

> Rupture, mischief, volatility. 
**Domain:** passion, the twist, the swap, the break. 
**Use:** bend outcomes, skew odds, open small ruptures that lead to new paths. 

## △ Drun — Fortitude · Green

_Titles: The Verdant Bastion · The Rooted Mountain · They-Who-Endure · The Stone Beneath All_

> Strength, endurance, the will to persist. 
**Domain:** growth, cycles, keeping on. 
**Use:** bind to cycles, turn pain into fuel, keep going and grow back stronger. 

## ⌘ Veyra — Charm · Purple

_Titles: The Veiled Smile · The Violet Thread · They-Who-Entangle · The Perfumed Mask_

> Social, kinship, the pull between beings. 
**Domain:** desire, bonds, alignment. 
**Use:** tie and untie bonds, align groups, lean hearts with words and presence.

## The Star

Every mortal carries all five Founts as fragments, marked by a **five-pointed star** birthmark near the navel — one point per fragment, its position random at birth, drifting with practice. A star resting dead-center (the solar plexus) is **Centered**: balanced across all five.

## How the Founts interlock

- ᛄ Seron's absolutes clash with ϟ Zhark's ruptures.
- ∵ Orvik's patterns often rely on ᛄ Seron's definitions.
- △ Drun's endurance resists ⌘ Veyra's sway.
- ⌘ Veyra's tethers bend both ᛄ Seron's laws and ϟ Zhark's chaos.
