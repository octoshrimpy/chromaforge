# Money

| Coin        | Worth   |
| ----------- | ------- |
| rp (rupy)   | 20 gp   |
| gp (gold)   | 10 sp   |
| sp (silver) | 10 cp   |
| cp (copper) | smallest |

`1 rp = 20 gp = 200 sp = 2000 cp`

**Spirit Orbs** are not money. They drop on rare deaths and are consumed for Fount points. They cannot be bought.
