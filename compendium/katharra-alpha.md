# Katharra alpha content pool

Small ready-to-run pool for no-prep play. Canon facts come from adjacent Katharra wiki source files. **Play detail** is ChromaForge material added to make those facts runnable; it becomes true only in campaign using it.

## Canon boundary

- Founts are laws without agency. Give desires only to people and creatures.
- Vis'Rodan material belongs to existing Vis'Rodan / Hollow Crown campaign context. Reuse it without changing village history, raid, Hunter, or Elder.
- Krailgoat, cairnbeak, and shirrak beetle wiki records are marked **Suggested**, not locked final canon.
- Names for unnamed roles, exact travel distances, ordinary residents, encounter behavior, and game stats are safe ChromaForge additions.

## Region: High Deserts

### High Pass Village

**Canon:** Small lower-mountain settlement, population about 200, mostly miners and families with seasonal traders. Elder remembers old oasis route. Healer has treated travelers with fast-healing wounds, vitality, and strange dreams. Village has a five-Fount shrine with an empty sixth alcove and a letter-exchange/archive.

**Play detail:** Use as starting location. Archive keeper wants records open and accurate; mine foreman wants dangerous routes suppressed until caravans can be guarded. Primary Fount is rolled, not fixed.

Source: `../katharra.wiki/locations/visrodan_travel_sites.md`, lines 39–51.

### Old Oasis Road

**Canon:** Remote, exposed, unmaintained route marked by Rodani stones. Recent careful camps belong to Hunter. Krailgoats roam highlands and cairnbeaks pass overhead. Route leads toward Vis'Rodan.

**Play detail:** Two travel days from High Pass Village. Each day's encounter check can reveal one marker, camp trace, creature, or weather front. Marker translations remain clues, not random decoration.

Source: `../katharra.wiki/locations/visrodan_travel_sites.md`, lines 55–68.

### Vis'Rodan ruins

**Canon:** Remote oasis village built inside remains of ancient dead Leviathan. Villagers raised and educated Leviathan spawnlings in reciprocal relationship. Kingdom raid enslaved villagers; spawnlings died; ruins have stood abandoned about four decades. Remaining features include huge bones, iridescent mineral-rich soil, collapsed well, hearth, spring path, sealed cultural cache, warm bone wall, and Rodani inscription: “Left for those who come after.”

**Play detail:** Treat cache as major obstacle, not loot pile. Opening it safely requires understanding inscription and respecting survivors' claim. Do not make Leviathan bones generic monster remains.

Source: `../katharra.wiki/locations/visrodan.md`, lines 11–46.

## People

### High Pass elder — unnamed canon role

- **Canon:** Lived in village sixty years; remembers when oasis road stopped being used.
- **Want (play detail):** learn why recent travelers use road without bringing danger onto village.
- **Leverage:** route history, old marker rubbings, authority to open archive.
- **Reaction:** welcomes specific questions; closes down if party treats vanished travelers as treasure rumor.

### Healer-herbalist — unnamed canon role

- **Canon:** Treating travelers with fast healing, vitality, and strange dreams.
- **Want (play detail):** find one patient who fled toward quarry after waking frightened.
- **Leverage:** treatment, patient notes, private knowledge of route traffic.
- **Reaction:** helps first, asks hard questions second; refuses to expose patients to crowd.

### The Hunter — campaign-specific

- **Canon:** Vis'Rodan child who survived raid. Soft-spoken, practical, tender, and convinced party may be sacred children meant to complete interrupted Leviathan cycle. Wants Crown Gland pieces, party at Leviathan, metamorphosis, and civilization punished. Elder's witness and party's undeniable selfhood can shake him. Personal name is not locked.
- **Want at first meeting:** give practical help and learn whether party matches his belief; not start a fight.
- **Leverage:** wilderness survival, Vis'Rodan route, hidden camps, firsthand memory.
- **Reaction:** patient with disagreement; becomes dangerous when choices narrow near gland or Leviathan.
- **Constraint:** Do not introduce a personal name from him. Name must come from Elder or another remnant witness.

Source: `../katharra.wiki/entities/sentients/the_hunter.md`.

### Vis'Rodan Elder — campaign-specific

- **Canon:** Raid survivor in seventies or eighties; knows village history, Hunter as child, diaspora, and cache. Their testimony can challenge Hunter. Name remains GM-assigned.
- **Want (play detail):** have cache recovered without turning culture into relic or weapon.
- **Leverage:** exact cache location, standing with Hunter, lived interpretation of Rodani practice.
- **Reaction:** tests whether party can repeat what villagers wanted rather than what outsiders imagine.

Source: `../katharra.wiki/entities/sentients/vis_rodan_elder.md`.

## Creatures

Stats use [alpha enemy bands](../guide.md#enemies-and-encounter-size). Wildlife usually wants safety, food, territory, or offspring protected—not a fight to death.

### Krailgoat

- **Canon:** Multi-horned cliff herbivore leaving nutrient-rich trails; seen on Old Oasis Road.
- **Behavior (play detail):** herd blocks narrow path while licking mineral seam. Retreats if given room; charges only when crowded or young approached.
- **Stats:** capable, HP 10, Defense 10, horn `d8 + 1`.
- **End combat:** party backs out of lane, offers mineral-rich vegetation, or opens alternate route.
- **Lead:** fresh green growth follows herd trail toward water.

### Cairnbeak

- **Canon:** Migratory carrion bird; seen overhead on Old Oasis Road.
- **Behavior (play detail):** circles recent death and follows wounded travelers. Lands only when safe.
- **Stats:** fragile, HP 6, Defense 8, beak `d6`; groups use swarm rule.
- **End combat:** move away from carcass, cover blood scent, or frighten flock once.
- **Lead:** flight circle marks a hidden carcass, camp, or downed traveler.

### Shirrak beetle

- **Canon:** Desert-and-oasis scavenger feeding on bone and carrion. Exact Vis'Rodan placement is generated, not canon.
- **Behavior (play detail):** strips loose dead material, including exposed old bone; curls and clicks when threatened.
- **Stats:** fragile, HP 6, Defense 8, mandibles `d6`; two beetles act as one group attack.
- **End combat:** stop touching feeding site, distract with carrion, or isolate desired object.
- **Lead:** beetles expose seams between natural bone and worked cache cover.
