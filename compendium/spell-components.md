# Spell components

Draft for mid-to-late game. Players, or a powerful wizard working for them, combine components into new spells and potions. Nothing here is available at session 0; the [spells](spells.md) list is what starting characters use.

## Build a spell

A custom spell has one or two **Effects**, an optional **medium**, and three settings: **Target**, **Reach**, and **Duration**. Add the costs to get the base mana cost. The guide's Magic rules then apply as usual: wheel distance adds mana, Fount score 4+/6+ and practice lower it, never below 1 (never below 2 for a base-3 spell).

1. **Effect.** What the spell does. Its Fount is the spell's Fount, for wheel distance and Fount reductions.
2. **Second Effect** (optional). A second Effect from a different Fount. Both really work, and the base cost is the two Effects added together. Write it "Steady and Hold". Two Effects is the limit.
3. **Medium** (optional, free). How the spell shows up: sound, vibration, light, scent, warmth, memory. A medium is flavor and adds no ability. Write it "Steady via sound".
4. **Target, Reach, Duration.** Defaults are free; stretching them costs mana.
5. **GM check.** The GM checks that the result stays within what the Effects fundamentally do. Anything beyond that is not possible.

## Target, Reach, Duration

| Setting  | Default (free)                                      | Stretch                                                                                |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Target   | you, one willing creature, or one unattended object | a group (+1), or a nearby zone (+1)                                                    |
| Reach    | touch or nearby (about 30 ft)                       | far, within sight (+1)                                                                 |
| Duration | 5 minutes                                           | instant (free, for a one-off), until the next rest (+1), until the next long rest (+2) |

An unwilling target makes the cast hard, as in the guide's Magic section. A group or zone with an unwilling creature in it does the same.

## Examples

- **Conceal steps** (Clemon's idea): Steady (Drun, 1) via vibration (free) + one willing ally (free) + until the next rest (+1). **Base cost 2.** You quiet the vibrations an ally makes as they walk, even a big one. It hides them from sound and from vibration senses such as an ommel's, but not from sight.
- **Lure**: Draw (Veyra, 1) via resonance (free) + far (+1) + instant. **Base cost 2.** A sound or tremor rings somewhere you can see and pulls attention to it.
- **Quiet pass:** Steady (Drun, 1) via sound (free) on you, for 5 minutes. **Base cost 1.** It hushes a small noise, such as a cough or a fart. Which sound, and when, is the player's call; a `d4` for timing is table fun, not a rule.
- **Hush:** Steady (Drun, 1) and Hold (Seron, 1) on you, for 5 minutes. **Base cost 2.** Two Effects at work: Steady settles the noise and Hold keeps it inside you.

An ancestry trait is always on and costs nothing. A spell can do for others what a trait does for its owner, such as quieting steps, but the trait is not a spell.

## Learning and making

- **Learn components through play.** Components are found, taught, or studied. Each Effect is learned on its own, and media are free to use, and you can only combine ones you know.
- **Design a spell.** Takes a few days of downtime. Make a hard `∵ Orvik` check. On success the spell is yours. On a failure the design needs another attempt or costs +1 mana until fixed, and a natural 1 gives the GM a consequence.
- **Hire a wizard.** A powerful wizard can design a spell for a character who knows its components: the price is the spell's base cost in gp, times 10, and a few days. Wizards will not teach a spell that works against their own interests.
- **Learn a custom spell.** A designed spell counts as known. Practice it like any other.

## Potions

A potion holds one spell's effect for whoever drinks or applies it. The drinker pays no mana and rolls no cast check, and the effect uses the spell's default settings. The potion is gone when used.

- **Brew.** Needs an alchemy kit (see [items](items.md)), a few hours of safe work, and the spell's cost plus 1 in mana. One potion per brewing.
- **Reagents.** Real ingredients worth the potion's price, found or bought.
- **Price.** `5 gp` per point of the spell's base cost. A healing draught (heals `d6`, nothing above max) is the model: 5 gp.
- **Limits.** A potion cannot carry a hard cast against an unwilling target; it works only on someone who takes it willingly. Stretched settings cost the same extra mana as for a cast.
