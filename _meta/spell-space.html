    Main role by system

    Share of each system's spells (tier 1–3) led by that role.

    Each spell is placed by how much it does for attack (damage or shutting a foe down), defense (protecting or healing), and utility (anything else), each scored 0–3.

    Chromaforge (filled circles, colored by Fount): scored by hand from PLAYER-HANDBOOK.md. Tier is base mana cost. Cantrips sit at utility 1 by rule.

    Other systems (hollow shapes): scored automatically by keyword rules in _meta/spell-space-data.py, so expect misfires. Tiers: D&D levels 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–9; Pathfinder ranks 1–3 / 4–7 / 8–10; Cairn has no levels, so every spellbook is tier 1.
