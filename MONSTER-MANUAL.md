# ChromaForge monster manual

Creatures of Katharra, ready to run. Lore comes from the Katharra wiki; behavior and stats are ChromaForge play details. Wildlife wants safety, food, territory, or its young protected, not a fight to the death.

Each entry has six lines:

- **Behavior:** what it is doing when found, and what makes it fight.
- **Stats:** tier, size, HP, armor, attack. Built like a character: size sets the HP dice, the natural weapon sets the attack die, tier adds the bonus (see Threat tiers).
- **Founts:** like an ancestry, a boon (the Fount its body and habits express) and a bane (the Fount it resonates with least). Creatures do not cast; the boon colors its tricks, and the bane hints at what turns it.
- **Traits:** what its body lets it do, like an ancestry's notes.
- **End combat:** two or three ways the fight stops short of death.
- **Lead:** what following it, or studying it, reveals.

Some add a named line for a trick of their Fount or biology. Conditions (prone, restrained, frightened, dazed, blinded, poisoned) are in the GM handbook.

**Swarm rule:** fragile creatures in numbers act as one group: one damage roll against one target per turn, and each damaging hit on the group removes one creature.

## Creatures

### Bramilla

Small burrowing mammal with a coat of hardened spine-clusters like bramble thorns. Harmless unless cornered.

- **Behavior:** roots at the edge of its burrow and freezes when seen. Fights only when cornered or grabbed.
- **Stats:** fragile, small, HP `2d4`, armor 1 (spines), spines `d4`.
- **Founts:** boon `ᛄ Seron`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** burrows through loose earth at walking pace.
- **Spines:** anyone who grabs or strikes it bare-handed takes `d4`, minus armor.
- **End combat:** leave it a way back to its burrow, step away, or throw a cloak over it.
- **Lead:** its burrows run under thorn thickets that hide paths and small caches.

### Bumbleboar

Stout, buzzing boars with bee-like wings that hover clumsily over fields, sometimes bumping into adventurers.

- **Behavior:** grazes and drifts over flowering fields in a loose sounder. Charges when its wallow or young are crowded, or when struck by accident during a collision.
- **Stats:** capable, medium, HP `3d4 + 3`, armor 1, tusks `d6 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** flies, slowly and badly; a strong wind grounds it.
- **Clumsy flight:** a bumbleboar that charges from the air knocks its target prone, hit or not, and lands prone itself.
- **End combat:** smoke calms the sounder, step out of its flight path, or leave it a patch of blossoms.
- **Lead:** sounders follow nectar to flowering fields, farms, and orchards.

### Cairnbeak

Migratory carrion bird that travels seasonal routes.

- **Behavior:** circles recent death and follows wounded travelers. Lands only when safe.
- **Stats:** fragile, small, HP `2d4`, armor 0, beak `d4`; groups use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `△ Drun`.
- **Traits:** flies; smells blood from a mile off.
- **End combat:** move away from the carcass, cover the blood scent, or frighten the flock once.
- **Lead:** a flight circle marks a hidden carcass, camp, or downed traveler.

### Cindral

Slow-moving snail with flame-tipped antennae. Leaves a faintly warm, ash-dusted trail wherever it goes.

- **Behavior:** creeps toward warmth. Never chases; it is more hazard than foe.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 1 (shell), antennae `d4` fire, only to someone who touches or attacks it in reach.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `⌘ Veyra`.
- **Traits:** unhurt by heat and fire; climbs sheer surfaces, slowly.
- **Ember trail:** its ash trail lights dry tinder at a touch, wet or not.
- **End combat:** walk away; it cannot follow.
- **Lead:** trails lead toward heat: a hot spring, a vent, a fire-touched place.

### Denonia

Den-dwelling ambush predator. Rarely ventures far from its burrow; lures prey with a low, repeating thrumming call.

- **Behavior:** thrums from the dark of its den and waits. Strikes whatever comes within reach of the entrance.
- **Stats:** dangerous, medium, HP `3d4 + 6`, armor 1, jaws `d6 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** sees in the dark; its thrum carries through walls and stone.
- **Ambush:** the first creature to reach the den mouth is hit before anyone acts. It never goes more than a few strides from the entrance.
- **End combat:** stay out of reach, feed it, or block the den mouth.
- **Lead:** bones and lost gear of lured prey lie around the den.

### Drudge

Stocky pack beast with a broad back and plodding gait. Docile under load; stubborn when unencumbered.

- **Behavior:** a loose drudge stands in the road and will not move. Fights only when whipped or goaded.
- **Stats:** capable, large, HP `4d4 + 3`, armor 1, kick `d6 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** carries a heavy load without slowing; cannot be shoved by anything its size or smaller.
- **End combat:** put a load on its back, lead it with food, or wait it out.
- **Lead:** a loose drudge still in harness means a caravan or camp in trouble nearby.

### Fezzle

Small erratic creature that throws harmless sparks when startled. Travels in loose, noisy clusters.

- **Behavior:** a cluster tumbles through brush, squeaking. Startled, it bursts into sparks and scatters.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, nip `d4`; clusters use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `ᛄ Seron`.
- **Traits:** unhurt by its own sparks or ordinary flame.
- **Sparks:** a startled cluster lights dry tinder and gives away anyone hiding near it.
- **End combat:** hold still and quiet, or let them pass.
- **Lead:** a cluster fleeing in sparks means a predator close behind it.

### Gnomad

A tiny wanderer with a knack for setting up temporary illusory shelters that confuse trespassers.

- **Behavior:** camps where it pleases and wants to be left alone.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, walking stick `d4`.
- **Founts:** boon `ᛄ Seron`, bane `⌘ Veyra`.
- **Traits:** never lost: always knows the way back along any route it has walked.
- **False shelter:** its camp looks like an empty hut or cave. Walking in turns a trespasser around and out the other side; a hard `∵ Orvik` check sees through it.
- **End combat:** leave a gift, back out of its camp, or show you see through the illusion.
- **Lead:** gnomads wander old routes and know the safe camps along them.

### Grubbler

A thief that steals from inventory on melee attack.

- **Behavior:** shadows travelers, then darts in when someone is distracted.
- **Stats:** fragile, small, HP `2d4`, armor 0, claws `d4`. A stronger grubbler is capable, small, HP `2d4 + 3`, armor 1, claws `d4 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `ᛄ Seron`.
- **Traits:** advantage on hiding in clutter, cramped, or dark spaces; climbs as fast as it runs.
- **Thief:** instead of dealing damage, a hit takes one carried item. A stronger grubbler can take worn armor or an item in hand.
- **End combat:** let it keep the item, trade it something shiny, or corner it so it drops its loot.
- **Lead:** its hoard holds other travelers' stolen belongings.

### Knibblet

Badger-sized lichen grazer of the tundra and frozen plains that chews a perfect ring around its range.

- **Behavior:** grazes along the edge of its ring and rears, chittering, at anyone who steps over it. Never leaves the ring and never lets anything stay inside it.
- **Stats:** capable, small, HP `2d4 + 3`, armor 1 (dense fur), teeth `d6 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `ᛄ Seron`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** digs through frozen earth; unbothered by cold.
- **Ring:** inside its ring a knibblet cannot be frightened or driven off. It never follows anyone out.
- **End combat:** step back over the ring, go around it, or leave lichen at the edge.
- **Lead:** rings cluster where the snow lies shallow, around warm ground and shelter. An empty ring means its knibblet was taken, and something hunts there.

### Krailgoat

Multi-horned, cliff-dwelling herbivore that leaves nutrient-rich trails.

- **Behavior:** a herd blocks a narrow path while licking a mineral seam. Retreats if given room; charges only when crowded or when its young are approached.
- **Stats:** capable, medium, HP `3d4 + 3`, armor 1, horn `d8 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** sure-footed on cliffs and broken ground; climbs near-sheer rock.
- **End combat:** back out of the lane, offer mineral-rich plants, or open another route.
- **Lead:** fresh green growth follows the herd's trail toward water.

### Lodelark

Bird that nests near ore veins and cave entrances. Its feathers have a faint metallic sheen; miners consider it good luck.

- **Behavior:** sings from rock ledges. Dives only at someone near its nest.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, beak `d4`.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** flies; hears hollow rock and loose ore behind a wall.
- **Miner's luck:** killing one in front of miners turns them against you.
- **End combat:** step back from the nest.
- **Lead:** nests mean an ore vein or a cave entrance close by.

### Marcove horn bend

Four-legged bird rumored to cure any ailment by touch, with a vivid peacock-like tail. Lays octahedron-shaped eggs. Found in highlands and ethereal zones.

- **Behavior:** rare and shy; flees at first sight. Fights only for its nest.
- **Stats:** capable, medium, HP `3d4 + 3`, armor 1, talons `d6 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `⌘ Veyra`.
- **Traits:** four-legged: outruns anything on two legs; glides short hops from high ground.
- **Healing touch:** a horn bend that trusts someone can touch them once to end one condition or disease. It cannot be forced.
- **End combat:** back away from the eggs, or let it flee.
- **Lead:** poachers hunt it for its eggs and tail; their trail and the nest are both close.

### Midgemire

Cloud of biting midges that rises off stagnant mire in the wetlands, heavy with clinging marsh gas.

- **Behavior:** hangs low over still water at dusk and boils up around anything that breaks the surface.
- **Stats:** capable, large (a cloud of tiny midges), HP `4d4 + 3`, armor 0, bites `d4 + 1`. One cloud is one creature. Weapons deal only 1 damage to it; fire, smoke, and strong wind deal full damage.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `ᛄ Seron`.
- **Traits:** flies; fills a zone and passes through any gap but a sealed one.
- **Flare:** fire set into the cloud burns it away at once and deals `d6` fire to everyone in its zone.
- **End combat:** get out of its zone and away from the water, shut yourself in a closed tent or net (it cannot cross a sealed line), or smoke it out.
- **Lead:** a midgemire marks foul, still water: undrinkable, and often something dead beneath it.

### Salamandarin

Bright orange salamander that gives off a citrus scent, luring predators and then escaping them.

- **Behavior:** basks on wet rock. Startled, it sprays scent and slips away.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, bite `d4`.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `△ Drun`.
- **Traits:** swims as fast as it walks; climbs wet rock.
- **Lure scent:** the spray clings to whoever startled it. Until washed off, nearby predators come for them first.
- **End combat:** let it go; it always tries to escape.
- **Lead:** a strong citrus smell means salamandarins, and the predators that hunt them.

### Shirrak beetle

Desert-and-oasis scavenger that feeds on bone and carrion.

- **Behavior:** strips loose dead material, old bone included; curls and clicks when threatened.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, mandibles `d4`; two beetles act as one group attack.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `⌘ Veyra`.
- **Traits:** burrows through sand; goes weeks without water.
- **End combat:** stop touching the feeding site, distract them with carrion, or set the object they want apart.
- **Lead:** the beetles expose seams between natural bone and a worked cache cover.

### Skithfish

Small schooling fish of the wetlands that leap between puddles.

- **Behavior:** a school flickers from puddle to puddle. Harmless; it only nips if scooped up.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, nip `d4`; schools use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `△ Drun`.
- **Traits:** breathes water, and air for a minute; leaps a zone between puddles.
- **End combat:** put them back in the water.
- **Lead:** a school's puddle path traces firm ground and fresh water through a swamp.

### Spindlebeast

Long-legged desert spinner that strings taut threads between stones across dunes and canyon mouths. Its young are "spindlecreeps".

- **Behavior:** waits at the hub of its lines, still as a dead shrub, and feels every strand. Comes when one is crossed; fights to the death only beside its egg sac.
- **Stats:** dangerous, large, HP `4d4 + 6`, armor 1, bite `d8 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `ᛄ Seron`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** climbs sheer rock and walks its own strands; feels movement along any strand it touches.
- **Lines:** crossing a strand tells it exactly where you are; spotting strands first is a risky `∵ Orvik` check. Instead of attacking, it can string a zone: anyone leaving it must spend an action cutting free or fall prone.
- **Spindlecreeps:** tearing the egg sac spills its young: fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, bite `d4`; they use the swarm rule.
- **End combat:** step back behind its outermost line (it never hunts past it), burn the strands, or leave the egg sac alone.
- **Lead:** its strands run between the desert's sheltered places, and old wrapped bundles in them hold travelers' gear.

### Stranglemaw

Camouflaged vine-serpent of the tropical rainforests that mimics the scent of fruit to attract prey.

- **Behavior:** hangs unseen among vines, smelling of ripe fruit, and waits for a hand to reach in.
- **Stats:** dangerous, large, HP `4d4 + 6`, armor 1, constrict `d6 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** climbs; standing still among vines, it counts as hidden.
- **Coil:** its first hit leaves the target restrained, and each of its turns deals damage to them until they break free.
- **End combat:** cut the victim free, burn or smoke it out, or leave its patch.
- **Lead:** a fruit scent with no fruit in sight.

### Tallowtail

Small golden-brown songbird with a pale, waxy fan tail. Common near settlements; drawn to lantern light after dark.

- **Behavior:** flocks around lights at night. Never fights; it is a nuisance.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, peck `d4`; flocks use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `ᛄ Seron`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** flies; sees well by lamplight, poorly in full dark.
- **Lantern flock:** a flock circling a camp's light can be seen from far off.
- **End combat:** put out the light.
- **Lead:** after dark, flocks gather over lit places: a settlement, a camp, a lamp someone left burning.

### Thritch

Wiry marsh predator of the wetlands that moves in sudden twitches; nobody, the thritch included, knows where it lunges next.

- **Behavior:** stalks reed beds and strikes the nearest thing that moves, then thrashes.
- **Stats:** dangerous, medium, HP `3d4 + 6`, armor 1, claws `d6 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `△ Drun`.
- **Traits:** swims as fast as it walks; moves through reeds without a sound.
- **Erratic:** at the start of its turn roll `d6`: on 1 it bolts to another zone, on 2–5 it attacks, on 6 it attacks twice.
- **Burnout:** after three rounds of fighting it tires and flees.
- **End combat:** outlast it, stay out of the reeds, or throw it something that moves.
- **Lead:** marsh birds go silent where one hunts, and its kills are left half-eaten and scattered.

### Thrumble

Heavy burrowing beast of the tundra that hums through ice and frozen ground, reading the echoes to map everything above it.

- **Behavior:** hunts beneath the frost, listening, and bursts up under whatever moves alone.
- **Stats:** dangerous, large, HP `4d4 + 6`, armor 1, jaws `d8 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `⌘ Veyra`.
- **Traits:** burrows through frozen ground at walking pace; eyeless, so darkness, light, and illusions mean nothing to it.
- **Groundsense:** it knows where everyone moving on the ground nearby is. Anyone standing still is lost to it.
- **Breach:** the first time it surfaces under a zone, everyone there makes a risky `△ Drun` check or falls prone.
- **End combat:** stand still until it loses you, cross onto bare rock, or drum a false rhythm away from the party.
- **Lead:** hollow-sounding ground marks its tunnels, which lead to buried things it heard and dug toward: caches, old camps, the frozen dead.

### Timberback

Moose-sized herbivore of the temperate forests that shapes glades by knocking down trees.

- **Behavior:** shoulders trees over to reach their leaves. Territorial in rut and when its calf is near.
- **Stats:** dangerous, large, HP `4d4 + 6`, armor 1, antlers `d8 + 2`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** cannot be shoved or knocked down by anything its size or smaller; pushes through thick forest at a run.
- **Treefall:** instead of attacking, it can topple a tree onto a nearby zone: `d6 + 2` to everyone there, minus armor, and the zone is blocked.
- **End combat:** give way, never stand between it and its calf, or drive it off with loud noise.
- **Lead:** fresh glades and fallen trees mark its route; its clearings make good camps.

### Veloom

Ferret-like creatures with long, flexible bodies covered in reptilian scales of brown, copper, rust, or muted gold, with expressive faces, big eyes, and prominent whiskers. Small enough to ride a suit of armor's shoulder. Cold-blooded: they need warmth to stay active and slow down as it gets cold. Before winter they steal soft things (socks, shirts, bedrolls, blankets, scarves, pelts) to line the shared dens where they lay eggs and wait out the cold. More interested in a warm sock than a jewel; troublesome, never malicious.

- **Behavior:** a small group works a camp together at dusk: one draws notice while the others slip off with anything soft and run for the den. Fights only when cornered or when its den or eggs are threatened.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 1 (scales), claws `d4`; groups use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `△ Drun`.
- **Traits:** squeezes through any gap its head fits; climbs as fast as it runs.
- **Thief:** instead of dealing damage, a veloom's hit can take one soft item the target carries or wears; the veloom then flees with it.
- **Cold:** in cold weather a veloom acts after everyone else and cannot outrun anyone. Near freezing it goes torpid and can be picked up.
- **End combat:** give up a soft item, leave its territory, or offer warmth (a fire, a pocket, a warm meal) to one of them.
- **Lead:** a trail of loose fibers leads to a den lined with stolen clothing, sometimes with a missing traveler's belongings among it.
- **Befriending:** a veloom can grow attached to someone who keeps it warm and fed. Animated armor, with no body heat to steal from it and plenty of room on its shoulders, is a favorite.

### Vuun

Bison with a rhino horn, used for farm labor.

- **Behavior:** a working vuun or a runaway, grazing or panicking. Charges when cornered or spooked.
- **Stats:** capable, large, HP `4d4 + 3`, armor 1, horn `d8 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** hauls heavy loads; cannot be shoved by anything its size or smaller.
- **End combat:** a farmer's call calms it, so does food, or simply let it pass.
- **Lead:** its brand names the farm it came from.

### Whiffle

Cute but mischievous air creature that cannot be hit until trapped.

- **Behavior:** tugs hats, snuffs candles, tangles hair. Has no real attack; it pranks.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, no damage.
- **Founts:** boon `ϟ Zhark`, bane `ᛄ Seron`.
- **Traits:** flies; rides any breeze and slips through any draft.
- **Slippery:** attacks and harmful spells always miss it until it is trapped: netted, boxed, sealed in, or held by magic.
- **End combat:** ignore it, give it something to play with, or trap it.
- **Lead:** whiffles gather where air moves; a whiffle pestering a wall means a draft, and a hidden passage behind it.

### Whimblight

Haze-creature of the ethereal zones that mimics actions after a brief delay.

- **Behavior:** drifts after travelers, copying them a beat late, harmless until someone attacks it.
- **Stats:** capable, medium (a drifting haze), HP `3d4 + 3`, armor 0, no attack of its own.
- **Founts:** boon `∵ Orvik`, bane `ϟ Zhark`.
- **Traits:** drifts through gaps like fog; cannot be grabbed, carried, or knocked down.
- **Echo:** each turn it repeats the last action taken against it, back at whoever took it, with the same damage roll or effect.
- **End combat:** stop acting against it and it fades, leave the haze, or give it something harmless to copy.
- **Lead:** its haze thickens where an ethereal zone is near.

### Whimwick

Satyr-goblin with a lit candle wick on its head. The flame changes color with its mood.

- **Behavior:** small bands camp and scavenge at night. Curious, quick to take offense, quicker to run.
- **Stats:** fragile, small, HP `2d4`, armor 0, knife `d4`; bands use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `ᛄ Seron`.
- **Traits:** sure-footed on broken ground; its wick lights its own zone dimly.
- **Mood flame:** the GM tells the table what color the flames burn, and so what the band feels.
- **End combat:** talk, offer a gift, or douse a wick: that one panics and flees.
- **Lead:** their flames show at night from far off and lead to their camp.

### Whirrel

Squirrel-like creature of the temperate forests that spins on its tail to dig, burying seed caches it mostly forgets.

- **Behavior:** whirs from cache to cache, chattering at intruders. Fights only when a cache is dug up.
- **Stats:** fragile, tiny, HP `1d4`, armor 0, bite `d4`; groups use the swarm rule.
- **Founts:** boon `△ Drun`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** climbs as fast as it runs; digs a cache-hole in moments.
- **Forgotten caches:** forgotten caches sprout, so a whirrel wood regrows fast after fire or felling.
- **End combat:** step away from the cache, or toss it a nut.
- **Lead:** saplings in neat clumps mark old caches; a freshly dug one means something hungry passed.

### Wogget

Big, soft-bellied amphibian of desert oases that bonds with whoever brings it water.

- **Behavior:** wallows in the shallows in a loose pod and waddles after anyone who splashes it. Fights when its pod is hurt or its waterhole fouled.
- **Stats:** capable, large, HP `4d4 + 3`, armor 1 (thick hide), slam `d6 + 1`.
- **Founts:** boon `⌘ Veyra`, bane `∵ Orvik`.
- **Traits:** swims; stores water in its belly and goes a week without drinking.
- **Imprint:** a wogget given water follows that person for a day, croaking, and the rest of its pod follows it. Stealth is out of the question.
- **End combat:** give it water, back away from the pool, or let it follow you.
- **Lead:** a pod on the move heads for the next water; in a drought, follow the woggets.
