Katharra Niche Grid

Five Fount regions, each split into 27 climate cells (temperature × moisture × altitude): 135 niches in all. Each number is how many of the 31 manual creatures live in that cell. A healthy community has about 15 species, from grubs to an apex hunter. Click any cell to see who lives there and which roles are missing.

Missing roles by region

Out of 27 cells in each region, the number of cells where nobody fills the role. 27 means the role is absent from the whole region.

Holes, worst first

  1. Swarm insects are almost absent: only the midgemire, and only in hot or temperate wet lowland. Every region needs a dry-country swarm, a cold one and a highland one. Seron and Orvik have none at all.
  2. Insectivores barely exist: one bramilla. Zhark and Veyra have none. Once swarm insects are added, something has to eat them.
  3. Aquatic life covers only wet lowland. Drun has no aquatic creature at all, and nothing lives in cold water or highland streams.
  4. Seron has no mid predator and no scavenger. The spindlebeast covers the hot desert, so cold and temperate Seron have nothing hunting between the small and apex tiers.
  5. Drun has no small predator and no carrion bird. The cairnbeak's bane is Drun, so Drun needs a carrion eater of its own.
  6. Veyra is the thinnest region, with 16 species and an average under 4 per niche. It has no burrower, decomposer, browser or small flier, and only the stranglemaw at the top, in hot wet lowland.
  7. Cold wet country is empty almost everywhere: bogs, fens and snowmelt marsh. Zhark cold wet lowland has nothing, and Seron hot wet highland (cloud forest) has nothing.
  8. Apex predators cover three patches: hot desert (spindlebeast), hot jungle lowland (stranglemaw) and cold tundra (thrumble). Nothing hunts at the top in temperate country, and the only highland apex is the spindlebeast in hot dry highland.
