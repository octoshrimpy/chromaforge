# Scratch ideas

Unsorted ideas not yet in any generator. V1 test characters (kobold monk, hippofolk druid, etterkin berserker, Lucy's warforged, stealthy necromancer fae) were dropped as obsolete builds. They're in git history at `ed39a6e`.

## Campaign hooks

- Locals hear [chants] by [ruins].
- [Queen] assassinated via [poisoning].
- [Dragon blood] cures [plague]. Party hired to collect some.
- [Powerful bard] hires the party to retrieve [magical instrument] from [dead wizard's coffin].
- Party finds [garbage bin] leading to [the seven hells].
- A party member's armor comes to life and tries to leave.
- Magical armor that promises great power.

## NPC encounter tables

Disposition (d10): 1 hostile on sight … 5 neutral … 10 friendly and social. Fill in 2–4 and 6–9.

Base emotion (d10): 1 joy, 2 trust, 3 fear, 4 surprise, 5 sadness, 6 disgust, 7 anger, 8 anticipation, 9 blank, 10 possessed.

## World tile extras

- Biomes: small archipelagos, enchanted mushroom forest.
- Content: giant statues, temple, mountain range, magical animals, hall of the dead (holds fallen PCs).

## Format idea

Tag entries so generators can filter by them, e.g. `Altar stone … _atop a hill_ _monster_ _NPC_`.
